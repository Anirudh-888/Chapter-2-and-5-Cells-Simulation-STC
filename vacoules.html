What Does the Vacuole Store?
💧
Water & Cell Sap
Maintains osmotic balance. Water enters by osmosis through the tonoplast, creating hydrostatic pressure inside the vacuole.
🧂
Minerals & Ions
K⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, NO₃⁻ stored for metabolic use. Acts as a mineral reservoir for the cell during growth.
🍬
Sugars & Organic Acids
Glucose, sucrose, amino acids stored as energy reserves and for maintaining concentration gradients.
🧹
Waste & Pigments
Metabolic waste, alkaloids, tannins, and anthocyanins safely isolated away from the sensitive cytoplasm.
Turgor Pressure Mechanism
Turgor Pressure: 65 kPa
01Water enters vacuole via osmosis across tonoplast membrane.
02Vacuole swells, pushing protoplasm against the rigid cell wall.
03Cell wall provides wall pressure back — creating net turgor pressure keeping the cell rigid.
04In drought: vacuole loses water, turgor drops, plant wilts — plasmolysis may occur.
Tonoplast Membrane
►Selectively permeable membrane surrounding the vacuole.
►Contains aquaporins (water channels) and ion transporters.
►Controls pH inside vacuole (usually acidic, pH 4–5).
►Contains V-ATPase pumps that acidify the vacuolar lumen.
